Take Action

Ask your legislator to end the clock change.

The fastest way to end Washington's biannual clock change is the Legislature — not a ballot signature. Enter your address to find your state senator and two representatives, then email or call them directly.

The ask: Please support a state path that ends seasonal clock changes using authority Washington already has, while preserving a transition to permanent daylight time if federal law changes. Please introduce and support the Last Fall Back Act in the 2027 legislative session.
